Frequently Asked Questions

What is the SilverBullet?

The SilverBullet is a universal tool for almost all locks using a disc-retainer principle.

It can open and decode 99% of disc-locks currently on the market.

It is a modular tool: it can be completely reconfigured depending on the lock type, profile and your preferences.

Tension can be applied at the front or back of the lock, and the tool comes with multiple tips for manipulating almost every type of disc-lock, including Abloy, Abloy Classic, Abloy Disclock, Abloy Profile, Anchor Las, ABUS..

The SilverBullet V2 is the result of over 10 years of research and development in the field of disc-retainer locks, and is recognised as the "ultimate" or "must have" tool for disc-lock systems.

What's the difference between the V1 and V2?

The SilverBullet 2 has a totally redesigned tip + tension system, and comes with a full kit of tips + tension tips, which allow for opening and decoding previously 'unpickable' locks: Abloy Disclock, Abloy Classic, Abloy Profile, Anchor Las, and so on.

I have the V1, do I need the V2?

If you want to work with Abloy, Anchor Las, Kensington or miniature disc mechanisms, you need the SilverBullet V2.

Can I use V2 tips on a V1?

Short answer: No.

Long answer: Although the V1 and V2 look similar, the SilverBullet V2 driver system was completely redesigned to meet the super-tight dimensions requirements of Abloy systems.

The tension + picking tip combined thickness is 2mm - allowing the tool to fit into the smallest of locks.

Because of this, the V2 tips are not compatible with the V1.

What is the guarantee on the SilverBullet?

All devices are hand-checked for defects, precision and 'feel' before leaving the factory; we can confidently guarantee that your SilverBullet will last a lifetime.
